These are examples of use. No formal colour specification or authoritative symbolism guide was located.

## Adopted application tokens

All coordinates are zero-based JPEG coordinates in the immutable project flyer. RGB values are sampled, not published RCCG hex specifications. JPEG compression can vary adjacent pixels.

| Role | Value | Evidence and use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand ink and poster typography | `#300066` | RCCG emblem centre, (245, 81), 20 occurrences in the centre sample region. Headings, poster frame, and dark surface. |
| Primary action and focus | `#027000` | RCCG emblem green lettering, (269, 97). Buttons, selected states, status and focus on light grounds. |
| Event accent and errors | `#B00000` | Official event lettering, (340, 700). Attendance ribbon and errors. |
| Paper and inverse text | `#FFFFFF` | White dove pixels observed inside (230, 79)–(269, 111), inclusive. Light surface and inverse foreground. |

Secondary, border, hover, disabled, and shadow treatments use the same four colours with semantic aliases or alpha. Approximate contrast on white is 15.9:1 for ink, 6.3:1 for green, and 7.4:1 for red. The interface uses white surfaces even under the operating system dark preference. Lanyard highlights and hardware shading derive from the same palette. Ink and green are not paired for text. No decorative gold, champagne, beige, cream, bronze, teal, orange, charcoal, borrowed Cairo colours, or arbitrary gradients are used. The intact flyer supplies the original imagery. Exported cards sample two native, unrecoloured regions from the same file for the official mark plate and the footer scenery. Neither mark is redrawn.
