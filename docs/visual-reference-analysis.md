## Official flyer

The upper lockup pairs the circular RCCG logo with the 2026 Holy Ghost Congress crest and black uppercase church and event names. The crowned King and front-facing white horse dominate the center in radiant light. Gates, clouds, heavenly riders and the KING OF KINGS and LORD OF LORDS banners extend to both sides. Deep blue in the King's mantle balances the surrounding ivory and amber. The lower center carries the largest type, a stacked red three-dimensional serif theme with gold edges. The white footer uses large bold dates, centered ministering credit and a right-aligned venue block. A vertical website and social handle also run along the right edge.

The footer gives 7–12 December 2026, Pastor E. A. Adeboye and other anointed ministers of God, and Redemption City, KM 46 Lagos–Ibadan Expressway, Mowe, Ogun State, Nigeria. Future compositions must protect the top lockup, focal illustration, lower theme and footer. The homepage hero and showcase display the complete source. The showcase uses the flyer and explanatory copy to avoid presenting the prototype render as official artwork.

## Website concepts

- **Studio PNG.** Navy navigation with branding at left and Home, Create Poster, About HGC and FAQ at right. A gold CTA and large serif studio headline sit above a light workspace. A horizontal three-step strip leads to the left name and upload form, a large central preview and a narrow right format and editing rail. Rounded cards, open gutters, red step markers, a solid generation CTA and a separate download CTA give the form clear hierarchy.
- **Generator PNG.** Navy navigation with Home, Create Poster, About HGC and Help. The red and gold CTA, gold serif hero and brighter horizontal artwork establish the campaign hierarchy. An ivory workspace groups a narrow vertical step rail, name and upload form, framed poster preview and right-side download, rotate, crop, regenerate and reset controls. Sans-serif labels, dashed upload borders and ample padding distinguish fields from the preview.
- **Responsive interpretation for later milestones.** Preserve the preview's aspect ratio and keep editing and format controls next to it in reading order. On narrow screens, stack form, preview and controls, wrap the navigation and turn the step rail into a compact horizontal list. The references are desktop compositions and supply no authoritative mobile layout. The crossed-arms example is one pose only.
